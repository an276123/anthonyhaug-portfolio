How this site works
One source. Four ways in.
Every project here is stored once, as a plain record of facts. Each lens is a different interface over that same record. Pick a project in one lens, switch, and you are looking at the same thing.
Why build it this way? Interfaces are getting cheap. An executive wants the summary, a curious person wants to try it, a builder wants to inspect it, and an AI agent wants no interface at all. When the facts are structured once, each of them gets what they need and nothing is maintained four times. That is the kind of work I do.
